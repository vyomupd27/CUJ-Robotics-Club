ABOUT THE CLUB

Welcome to the CSE Robotics Club, Central University of Jammu

We're a community of students from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, School of Engineering and Technology, passionate about robotics and automation — powered in partnership with e-Yantra. Through hands-on projects, workshops and competitions like self-balancing bots, line followers and soccer-playing rovers, we explore real approaches to problem solving. Whether you're a first-time builder or a seasoned robotics enthusiast, our club gives you the space to learn, collaborate and push the boundaries of what a small team of students can build. Join us in shaping the future of robotics at CUJ.
